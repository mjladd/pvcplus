USAGE:

   amptodB [ flags ] amplitude(s)
	flags:
	(Values in brackets denote defaults.)
   :n	    normalization amplitude [1.]

   Converts amplitude value(s) to decibels.
   Specification of the -n flag (i.e. -n32000. ) causes the amplitude to first 
   be normalized to the -n range ( i.e. amplitude / 3200. ).
