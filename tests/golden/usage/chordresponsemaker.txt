chordresponsemaker:  chordal frequency response synthesizer
			(multiple harmonic tone format)  
			(values in brackets represent defaults)
chordresponsemaker   [flags]  [output freqresponse file]
	N:	fft length [1024]
	R:	sampling rate [44100]
	F:	(ascii) DATA FILE of unordered sextuples:
		     Each consisting of: 
		     (1) pitch (octave.pitchclass) or frequency
			   (Values of 12 or less are interpreted as 
				octave.pitchclass; otherwise, units are in Hertz.)
		     (2) number of partials (0 = all partials below Nyquist)
		     (3) bandwidth as proportion of fundamental frequency
			(Values of 1. or less are interpreted as proportions 
				of the fundamental frequency; otherwise, units are in Hertz.
		     (4) decibels
		     (5) partial spacing as proportion of fundamental frequency
		     (6) decibel rolloff (+ or -) per octave of partials 
	s:	Frequency Response Accumulation Method: [0]
		    (treatment of overlapping bin values)
		    0 = peak response (uses highest of all)
		    1 = sum response (uses sum of all)
	D:	Band Window Type: 0 = triangle,  1 = rectangle [0]
		    (As reflected in decibels)
	i:	filter type: bandpass (0),  band reject (1) [0]
	v:	frequency response stderr printout: high cutoff frequency in Hz [0]
		    (0 = off)
	a:	decibels spectrum plot file
	h:	usage
