impulseresponse:  impulse response analysis 
impulseresponse   [flags] [input sound file] [output impulseresponse file]
	b:	begin time in seconds  [0.] 
	e:	end time in seconds ( 0. = end of file) [0.] 
	C:	resynthesis channel (1 -> ?) (0 = all) [0] 
	d:	amplitude normalization level in decibels [0]
	N:       normalization:                [1]
	   0: Do not normalize. 
	   1: Normalize channels independently.
	   2: Normalize channels together against channel with peak amplitude.
	a:	Decibels Spectrum Plot File
	P:	FREQUENCY RESPONSE PRINTOUT: high cutoff frequency in Hz [0]
		     (0 = off)
